# MSORG public property discovery

This is a best-effort public-web research inventory, not a claim of completeness. A property is a verified lead only when an evidence page directly identifies MSORG as developer and publishes the client business's contact information. App names discovered through “More by MSORG” are candidates until their individual records are checked.

## Data handling

- Only publicly published business contact details are included.
- `whatsapp_status=not_verified` means a public phone exists but no explicit WhatsApp link has yet been verified.
- “Unknown” website values must not be guessed from the brand name.
- Contacts should be used for individualized B2B outreach with opt-outs respected, not unsolicited bulk messaging.
